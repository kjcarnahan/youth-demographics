Reading these numbers

  • ACS figures are survey estimates averaged over five years. Compare vintages five years apart (2019 vs 2024) for a clean read; adjacent years share four years of data.
  • For ZIP-sized areas the margin of error on a teen age band is often ±10% or more. Small year-to-year wiggles are noise.
  • Census ZIP areas (ZCTAs) follow ZIP boundaries, not county lines: 30024 and 30097 include parts of Gwinnett County.
  • Ages 12–14 and 15–17 stand in for middle and high school. They are close, not exact: many 6th graders are 11 and many seniors are 18.
  • School enrollment is a real annual count and the steadier yardstick. It only counts the public schools you tick, so it moves when schools open or attendance zones are redrawn (Denmark High opened in 2018, East Forsyth in 2021).
  • When a youth-group year is newer than the latest available data, the latest available figure is used and the row is marked.
