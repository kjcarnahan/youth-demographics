Reading these numbers

  • School enrollment is a real count taken every fall, so it is the steadier yardstick. It moves when schools open, close or have their attendance zones redrawn.
  • Census figures are survey estimates averaged over five years. Compare points five years apart for a clean read; neighboring years share four years of data.
  • For a single ZIP code the margin of error on a teen age band is often 10% or more. Small wiggles are noise.
  • Census ZIP areas follow ZIP boundaries, which can cross city and county lines.
  • Ages 12–14 and 15–17 stand in for middle and high school. Many sixth graders are 11 and many seniors are 18.
  • When your year is newer than the latest published data, the latest available figure is used and marked with an asterisk.
